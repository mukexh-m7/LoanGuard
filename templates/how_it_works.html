MODEL INPUTS

Factors used for prediction

LoanGuard evaluates nine customer and loan-related attributes.

01 Gender

Applicant demographic information.

02 Age

Applicant age in years.

03 Income

Applicant income information.

04 Loan Amount

Requested loan amount.

05 Loan Term

Duration of the requested loan.

06 Credit Score

Applicant credit profile.

07 Employment

Employment status information.

08 Marital Status

Applicant marital status.

09 Previous Defaults

Historical default information.
